E. coli removal is computed from colloid filtration theory (Tufenkji and Elimelech, 2004) for a 1 µm cell, the biochar grain size, porosity, depth and flow. α = 1 is the most favourable case (every collision sticks). HYDRUS-1D gave 3.7% removal at α = 1 and 0.4% at α = 0.1. Captured cells are held, not killed.
